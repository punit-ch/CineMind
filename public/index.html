What CineMind Can Do

AI that actually gets cinema

Not just an algorithm — a film-literate AI that understands themes, directors, storytelling styles, and your taste.

✦
Taste-based Recommendations

Enter your favorite films, directors, actors, and genres. Get 8 perfectly curated picks with detailed explanations for each.

🎭
AI Movie Critic

Deep analysis of any film: themes, strengths, weaknesses, cinematography, storytelling, and who should watch it.

💬
Movie Chat Assistant

Ask anything: "Recommend films like Parasite", "underrated 90s thrillers", "movies with unreliable narrators" — and get smart answers.

🌀
Mood-Based Discovery

Tell CineMind your current mood and get a curated selection from mind-bending to romantic, dark to motivational.

🎉
Movie Night Planner

Planning a movie night? Input group size and mood — AI picks the one film everyone will enjoy, with snack pairings and discussion starters.

⚡
Movie Comparison

Head-to-head comparison of two films across storytelling, direction, cinematography, performances, and emotional impact.

📊
Personal Taste Profile

AI analyzes your recommendation history to reveal your favorite genres, preferred era, storytelling style, and your cinematic blindspot.

❤️
Watchlist & History

Save movies, track your recommendation history, and revisit past discoveries — all persisted locally, no account required.
